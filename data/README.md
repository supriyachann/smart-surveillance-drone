# Sample data and provenance

`sample-events.json` contains two explicitly synthetic person/car events. They explain the event schema and drive the dashboard sample session. They are not model predictions and must not support accuracy claims.

`synthetic-scene.png` and `synthetic-scene.mp4`, when generated by `scripts/create_assets.py`, are original programmatically drawn illustrations. They show a schematic campus and synthetic bounding boxes, with a permanent synthetic-data label. The image is 1280x720; the clip is 10 seconds at 15 FPS. They contain no real people, locations or collected footage. They are distributed under the repository's MIT license. The optional detector is not expected to recognize these schematic figures reliably; they are UI/documentation fixtures, not an AI evaluation set.

Before evaluating real detection, provide authorized footage with provenance, license/consent, recording conditions, ground-truth annotations and a held-out test split. Keep identifiable footage, private locations and GPS metadata out of public commits unless publication is authorized.
